Algorithms Used

1. Common Neighbors: Counts common friends between two users.

2. Jaccard Similarity: Calculates the ratio of common neighbors to the union of their neighbors.

3. Adamic-Adar: Gives higher importance to shared neighbors with lower network degree.

Viva Explanation

What is Link Prediction?
Link prediction is a graph-based technique used to predict possible future relationships between currently unconnected users.

Why is it useful?
It can be used for friend recommendations, social network analysis and recommendation systems.

What does this project do?
The project calculates similarity scores between users and recommends users who may form future connections.
